# Privacy Policy — YouTube Speed Master

**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

YouTube Speed Master does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

## What is stored

Only your extension preferences (for example, your preferred playback speed) are stored,
using `chrome.storage.sync`. That data stays inside your Chrome profile and, if you are signed
in to Chrome, syncs through Google's own sync service. The extension has no servers and no
network requests of its own.

## What is not collected

- No personal information
- No browsing history
- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
- No advertising identifiers
- No remote code

## Permissions

- `storage` — saves your preferred playback speed locally.
- Content script on `youtube.com` — sets the HTML5 `<video>` element's `playbackRate` on
  YouTube pages. Nothing on those pages is read, stored or sent anywhere.
