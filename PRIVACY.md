# Privacy Policy — Speed Master

**Last updated:** 2026-11-06

Speed Master does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

## What is stored

Only your extension preferences are stored:

- `chrome.storage.sync` — preferred playback speed, pitch setting, custom preset speeds, panel
  appearance and keyboard options. This stays inside your Chrome profile and, if you are signed in
  to Chrome, syncs through Google's own sync service.
- `chrome.storage.local` — per-channel speeds you explicitly choose to remember. This stays on the
  device and is never synced or transmitted.

The extension has no servers and makes no network requests of its own.

## What is not collected

- No personal information
- No browsing history
- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
- No advertising identifiers
- No remote code

## Permissions

- `storage` — saves your preferred playback speed locally.
- Content script on `youtube.com` — sets the HTML5 `<video>` element's `playbackRate` on
  YouTube pages. Nothing on those pages is read, stored or sent anywhere.
