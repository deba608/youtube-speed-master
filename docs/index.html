Speed Master

0.25x – 4x playback for YouTube

A Chrome extension that unlocks playback speed beyond YouTube's built-in 2x cap, with a slider, presets and keyboard shortcuts. Pitch stays natural, ads stay at 1x.

  • Slider 0.25x – 4x (0.05x steps) and 11 presets
  • Shift + . / Shift + , to step by 0.25x
  • Default speed saved in chrome.storage.sync
  • No tracking, no analytics, no servers
