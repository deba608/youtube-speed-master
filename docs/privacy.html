Privacy Policy — Speed Master

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Speed Master does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

What is stored

Only your extension preferences are stored: preferred playback speed, pitch setting, custom presets, panel appearance and keyboard options in chrome.storage.sync, plus the per-channel speeds you choose to remember in chrome.storage.local. Synced data stays inside your Chrome profile and, if you are signed in to Chrome, syncs through Google's own sync service. Channel memory never leaves the device. The extension has no servers and makes no network requests of its own.

What is not collected

  • No personal information
  • No browsing history
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No remote code

Permissions

  • storage — saves your playback speed, pitch preference, custom presets and any per-channel speeds you choose to remember. Syncable settings use chrome.storage.sync; channel memory stays in chrome.storage.local.
  • Content script on youtube.com — sets the HTML5 <video> element's playbackRate on YouTube pages. Nothing on those pages is read, stored or sent anywhere.
